PROJECT OVERVIEW

FRENCH WINE ROOTED IN TRADITION, ELEVATED THROUGH PRESENTATION.

A premium rigid magnetic-door presentation box designed to house a French vineyard wine. The structure creates a prestigious unboxing experience, with double doors opening to reveal a standard-size wine bottle nestled within a suede-lined cushioned interior. The packaging combines protection, presentation, and material contrast to elevate the wine into a refined gifting experience.

04

DIELINE & STRUCTURE

A RIGID PRESENTATION SYSTEM DEVELOPED AROUND PROTECTION, OPENING & DISPLAY.

The structure is developed as a multi-component rigid box with two hinged doors that meet at the center, a magnetic closure, and an interior housing designed around a standard wine bottle. The dieline study focuses on panel relationships, fold and hinge locations, closure alignment, and the space required to secure and present the bottle.

01

STRUCTURAL SETUP

The package begins with the dimensions of a standard wine bottle and builds outward to establish the bottle housing, surrounding clearance, rigid outer shell, hinged doors, and magnetic closure.

02

MATERIAL DIRECTION

The concept is intended as a wrapped rigid-board presentation box. Rigid greyboard provides the structural body, while a paper wrap creates the finished exterior surface. Magnetic closures are positioned where the two front doors meet. Final board thickness and production specifications would be confirmed during manufacturing development.

WORK IN PROGRESS — Structural development is shown at its current stage. Final bottle branding, surface graphics, material specifications, and finished production artwork are still in development.
