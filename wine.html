PROJECT OVERVIEW

FRENCH WINE ROOTED IN TRADITION, ELEVATED THROUGH PRESENTATION.

A premium rigid magnetic-door presentation box designed to house a French vineyard wine. The structure creates a prestigious unboxing experience, with double doors opening to reveal a standard-size wine bottle nestled within a suede-lined cushioned interior. The packaging combines protection, presentation, and material contrast to elevate the wine into a refined gifting experience.

04

DIELINE & STRUCTURE

A RIGID PRESENTATION SYSTEM DEVELOPED AROUND PROTECTION, OPENING & DISPLAY.

The structure is developed as a multi-component rigid box with two hinged doors that meet at the center, a magnetic closure, and an interior housing designed around a standard wine bottle. The dieline study focuses on panel relationships, fold and hinge locations, closure alignment, and the space required to secure and present the bottle.

01

STRUCTURAL SETUP

The structure is developed around a standard 750 mL wine bottle, with clearance added for the custom bottle insert and protective cushioning. A central rigid housing secures the bottle while two magnetic hinged outer doors open from the center to create the presentation reveal. The door depth, hinge placement, insert fit, and magnetic closure positions are all coordinated to allow the package to close cleanly and hold the bottle securely in place.

02

MATERIAL DIRECTION

  • STRUCTURE: Rigid greyboard shell for the main body and hinged front doors.
  • EXTERIOR WRAP: Textured navy specialty paper with a refined matte finish.
  • LOGO FINISH: Blind debossed CR monogram across the front doors.
  • CLOSURE: Concealed magnetic closures positioned where the two doors meet.
  • INTERIOR INSERT: Custom shaped EVA foam insert designed to securely house the bottle.
  • INSERT COVERING: Navy microsuede wrap for a soft, premium interior finish.
