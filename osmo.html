PROJECT OVERVIEW

HOME FRAGRANCE REIMAGINED THROUGH CONTEMPORARY PACKAGING.

OSMO is a conceptual home fragrance packaging case study centered around a series of home fragrances presented through contemporary and experimental packaging systems. The project explores structural packaging, material contrast, translucent surfaces, debossed branding, and production dielines.

DIELINE & PRODUCTION

A MULTI-COMPONENT PACKAGING SYSTEM BUILT AROUND FORM, MATERIAL & INTERACTION.

The OSMO packaging system is documented through structural dielines, material specifications, production guides, and finishing details. Each component is developed to work together as a unified presentation while maintaining its own structural and material purpose.

01

PRODUCTION SETUP

Separate cut, fold, bleed, safe-area, and artwork guides are prepared for each structural component to support accurate production and assembly.

02

MATERIAL & FINISH

A frosted, tinted translucent plastic cover is paired with a 1.5 mm rigid greyboard base wrapped in smooth white uncoated paper, tactile debossed branding, and a contrasting product color to create a material-driven presentation.
