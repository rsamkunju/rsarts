PROJECT OVERVIEW

APPAREL & BRANDING DESIGN

PERFORMANCE STREETWEAR SYSTEM BUILT AROUND SPEED AND MOTION.

Velocyte is a conceptual motorsport apparel brand focused on technical garment design, custom jersey graphics, speed-driven visual systems, and production-ready apparel documentation. The project combines expressive brand direction with practical flats, artwork placement, front-and-back garment development, and tech-pack thinking.

02

BRAND IDENTITY

LOGO SYSTEM

A SHARP WORDMARK ENGINEERED TO FEEL FAST BEFORE IT EVEN MOVES.

Angled letterforms, extended terminals, and directional cuts create a wordmark that communicates acceleration and control.

The identity is designed to live across jersey fronts, woven labels, patches, garment trims, retail signage, and technical documentation. Supporting motion marks extend the same geometry into patterns and apparel graphics.

GRAPHIC SYSTEM

MOTION-INSPIRED PATTERNS

A FLEXIBLE GRAPHIC LANGUAGE BUILT FROM SPEED LINES, GRID SHIFTS, AND RACING CUES.

Instead of relying on a large color palette, Velocyte builds recognition through repeatable motion systems. The graphics can scale from subtle garment panels to full jersey artwork, retail environments, and campaign backgrounds.
