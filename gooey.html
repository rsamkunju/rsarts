PROJECT OVERVIEW

BRANDING & PACKAGE DESIGN

A WARM, PLAYFUL IDENTITY BUILT AROUND THE PERFECT MELT.

Gooey is a conceptual cookie brand inspired by a warm and melted cookie, bold dessert visuals, and the playful anticipation of the first bite. The identity combines custom dripping typography, chocolate inspired colors, colorful packaging, promotional campaigns, and physical customer touchpoints.

BRAND IDEOLOGY

BUILT FROM THE
DRIPS, SMILES, AND A SYMBOL.

The Gooey logo is inspired by the feeling of a warm, melty indulgence. Rounded letterforms communicate softness and playfulness, while exaggerated drips reflect the rich, gooey texture of the cookies.

A smile shaped silhouette brings the logo together, framing the Gooey letterforms like a mouthful of warm chocolatey delights. The same smile we guarantee you’ll have after every bite.
