GOOEY COOKIES

BRANDING AND PACKAGE DESIGN

Gooey is a conceptual cookie branding and package design project built around the idea of warm, melted-center cookies and playful dessert visuals. The branding combines custom display typography, dripping chocolate-inspired graphics, and modern bakery-inspired visuals to create an identity that feels both playful and indulgent. Designed to capture the comforting and crave worthy feeling of freshly baked cookies.
