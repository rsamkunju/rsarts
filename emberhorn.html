PROJECT OVERVIEW

BRANDING & PACKAGE DESIGN

A FIRE-FORGED IDENTITY BUILT AROUND SMOKE, HEAT, AND FLAVOR.

Emberhorn is a conceptual barbecue sauce brand built around smoky bourbon flavor, subtle heat, and open-flame cooking. The identity combines a horned fire emblem, distressed display typography, dark packaging, and dimensional product visualization to create a bold modern-rustic brand system.
