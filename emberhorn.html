EMBERHORN is a bold blend of smoky bourbon barbecue and rich homemade flavor, crafted for people who take their grilling seriously. Built with deep charred sweetness, subtle heat, and a slow-cooked taste, it brings a fire-kissed finish to everything from ribs and burgers to wings and pulled pork. Whether you’re smoking low and slow or grilling over open flame, Emberhorn delivers the perfect balance of sweet heat and smoky intensity.
