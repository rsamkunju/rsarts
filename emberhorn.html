The Emberhorn Bottle

Warm Ember is a sweet and smoky BBQ sauce with the first spark of heat, introducing the beginning of Emberhorn’s long road to bold, fiery flavor. The bottle was fully modeled, textured, and rendered in Blender 3D to showcase the product’s packaging, material finish, and shelf-ready presentation.

EMBERHORN HOT BBQ

BRANDING & PACKAGE DESIGN

EMBERHORN is a conceptual barbecue sauce brand built around smoky bourbon flavor and a subtle spicy kick. Inspired by slow-cooked barbecue and open flame grilling, the brand focuses on rich smoke, deep sweetness, and bold heat to create a strong fire-inspired identity. Emberhorn takes on a blend of modern rustic aesthetic.
