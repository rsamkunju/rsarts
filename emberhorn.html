EMBERHORN

Branding and Package Design

EMBERHORN is a conceptual barbecue sauce brand built around smoky bourbon flavor and a subtle spicy kick. Inspired by slow-cooked barbecue and open flame grilling, the brand focuses on rich smoke, deep sweetness, and bold heat to create a strong fire-inspired identity. From the packaging to the visual system, Emberhorn blends rustic barbecue culture with a modern, premium aesthetic.
