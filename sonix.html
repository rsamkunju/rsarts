PROJECT OVERVIEW

BRANDING / 3D / MOTION

A FUTURISTIC HARDWARE BRAND BUILT AROUND PERFORMANCE AND PRECISION.

Sonix is a conceptual gaming-peripherals brand exploring how next-generation hardware can look, move, and communicate. The project combines a clean technology-driven identity with custom product design, 3D visualization, animation, campaign assets, and a scalable retail system for headsets, keyboards, mice, and supporting accessories.

BRAND IDENTITY

LOGO SYSTEM

A CLEAN MARK DESIGNED TO FEEL FAST, TECHNICAL, AND BUILT FOR HARDWARE.

Rounded geometry, a controlled red accent, and a distinctive X give Sonix a recognizable identity without relying on excessive gaming clichés.

The primary logo is designed to work across product shells, packaging, screen graphics, social assets, and retail displays. Supporting linework and modular X-based graphics extend the system into motion and interface-inspired layouts.
